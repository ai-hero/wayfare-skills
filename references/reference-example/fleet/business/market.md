# Market

## Segments

| Segment | Who | Need |
| -- | -- | -- |
| households | neighbors doing an occasional job at home | one tool for one weekend, without buying it |
| makers | hobbyists who build and mend things most weeks | specialist tools they use often but cannot store |

## Offerings

| Offering | What | Segment |
| -- | -- | -- |
| tool-loans | a tool reserved online and lent for a week | households, makers |
| repair-help | a monthly evening where volunteers help fix a member's own tool | makers |
